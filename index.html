The calendar

This shows when Sage needs sitting and the days people have blocked out. To add days you can't do, open the shared Google Calendar (link below) and add an all-day event.

How to log a day you can't sit

  1. Open the shared calendar: add your calendar link here
  2. Tap the day you're not available and add an all-day event.
  3. Title it like Dan — can't sit so it's clear at a glance.
  4. It'll show up here automatically. Done.
